GitBridge is an independent, generic bridge connecting AI assistants to GitHub repositories. IME is the convention it supports first-class, not the definition of the product.

**Why:** The product owner ratified this distinction after a documentation pass accidentally linked public product copy to a private operational hub and risked over-generalizing deliberate IME conventions.

**How to apply:** Lead core product copy generically. Describe `session_bootstrap` as deliberately opinionated IME-convention support and do not parameterize its manifest until real non-IME demand appears. Public product/reference copy links to `ioTus/openIME`; spoke-governance files continue to point to the real private hub.
